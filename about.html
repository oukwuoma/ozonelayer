Who's behind the layer

Defense-grade thinking for everyday threats

Ozone Layer is a cybersecurity consulting practice built on a foundation of hands-on security engineering experience, bringing that same rigor to the businesses that need it most: small and medium-sized companies navigating CMMC and NIST 800-171 compliance.

Most cyberattacks target small businesses — not enterprises with dedicated security teams. That reality shapes our mission: put affordable, high-value security tools and expertise directly in the hands of SMBs, so compliance isn't just a checkbox but a real layer of protection.

What sets us apart is simple — we don't just advise, we build. Our compliance audits run on a custom-built scanning tool, tested against a real multi-system Windows and Linux environment, so every finding is backed by an actual technical assessment, not a generic checklist.

We specialize in NIST 800-171 and CMMC 2.0 compliance readiness, helping small businesses build the security foundation they need to compete for — and keep — the contracts that matter.
